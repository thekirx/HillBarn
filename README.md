## The five findings that shape everything

**1. The café and venue look like two different companies.**
Different logos, different palettes, no shared system. A couple who finds the windmill café on Facebook has no path to learning they can be married there. Unifying them — without flattening either — is the site's main job.

**2. Their 2026 design language is genuinely good, and nobody has put it on a screen.**
Recent posters are *editorial print*: cream paper, high-contrast serif, letterspaced caps kickers, circular seals, photographs inset with generous margins. That became the creative concept — **"Printed matter, on a hill."** Not imposed on them; extended from what they already do.

**3. Colours are sampled, not guessed.**

| Role | Hex | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ground | `#F3EFE4` | 71.7% of a 2026 poster background |
| Primary ink | `#221E15` | Headline ink, same poster (warm, not neutral) |
| Accent | `#895A3C` | 51.9% of a solid badge fill |
| Architecture | `#293742` | Sampled from the windmill roof |
| Events field | `#2A5657` | Flat ground of the Tasting Room graphic |

No barn-red, no sage-green — **neither appears anywhere in Hillbarn's identity.** The reds in their imagery are tomatoes and peppercorns.

**4. Voice: English, no emoji, proof-by-longevity.**
"Four years. One favourite." · "Made from scratch. No fillers added." Taglish appears only in *customer comments*, never in Hillbarn's own copy — so we recommend against writing it for them.

**5. The accredited-caterer model.**
Hillbarn provides the venue; food for large events comes from partners (Laurentino's, Juan Carlo, Town's Delight). No competitor explains this, and it confuses first-time clients — so explaining it plainly became a homepage section and a differentiator.

## Two constraints to flag

### The logo is the biggest gap
No SVG, no transparent PNG, nothing above 150px in public sources. The best obtainable is a **470×120 crop from one of Hillbarn's own posters.**

The logo was **not** traced, redrawn, or AI-generated. The mock will use a clearly-labelled typographic stand-in until Hillbarn supplies vector files.

### Photography is reference-grade, not production-grade
29 assets collected, but **only one exceeds 1000px** — the 2048×824 aerial, wide enough for a hero band but too short for a full-height one.

- **Zero interior photography** exists publicly, despite the second-floor windmill dining room being a signature feature.
- **Weddings rests on a single 513×640 image.**

Empty folders carry `_MISSING.md` notes rather than stock substitutes. A prioritised shot list is in `BRAND-ASSETS.md`.

## What is deliberately marked TBD

- **Opening hours** — three conflicting versions found, and Hillbarn's own graphic contradicts both directories.
- **Capacity** (~300) and **~5,000 sqm** — published by caterer partners, not by Hillbarn.
- **All pricing.** The ₱228,000 figure circulating online is a *caterer's* package held at Hillbarn, not a Hillbarn rate. The ₱88,000 venue rate was a promo that **expired 31 August 2021**. Neither is attributed to Hillbarn anywhere in these documents.
- **Typefaces.** Classified by style with recommended substitutes; no font is claimed as Hillbarn's without a vector file as proof.

Testimonials were dropped from the homepage: public sentiment is genuinely mixed (Tripadvisor 3.9/5, Top Gear PH 6/10 on food vs Restaurant Guru 4.5/5). Cherry-picking praise would be fragile. Verifiable proof replaces it — "Best seller since May 2022", the 114K following, named caterer partners.
