**Research date:** 5 September 2026
**Researcher note:** All information below is from **public sources only**. No accounts were created and no logins were used. Instagram and Facebook were read in a logged-out state, which caps how many posts are visible (Instagram shows 12; Facebook shows a partial feed).

## 1. Business identity — VERIFIED

Hillbarn in Tagaytay is **one property operating two public-facing brands**:

| Brand arm | What it is | Primary handle |
|---|---|---|
| **Hillbarn Café & Restaurant** | Dutch-barn/windmill themed café + restaurant | `@hillbarncaferestaurant` |
| **Hillbarn Tagaytay Events Place** | Barn-themed events/wedding venue | `@hillbarn_tagaytay` |

**How this was verified:** both arms publish the **same street address** — *Daang Luma Road, Barangay Kaybagal North, Tagaytay City, Cavite*. Philippine Primer gives this address for the café; Juan Carlo and Window Seat give the same address for the events place. Third-party coverage explicitly links them: the events-venue amenity list includes "a cafe," and Juan Carlo describes the venue as having an "adjacent Dutch-inspired café and restaurant with a windmill structure."

**Disambiguation — businesses that are NOT this Hillbarn.** Searches surface several unrelated entities with similar names; none were used as evidence:

- **Hillbarn Theatre** (Foster City, California, USA) — community theatre.
- **Harper Hill Ranch**, **Hill Prince**, **The Emerald Hill** — US wedding venues listed on Zola, surfaced by "hill barn wedding venue" queries.
- Generic "hill/barn" wedding venues on Travelocity and Zola listings.

⚠️ **Naming inconsistency (real finding, not an error):** the business writes its own name three ways in public material — `Hillbarn` (logo, Facebook, Instagram), `Hill Barn` (one Instagram Reel cover: "THE HILL BARN TAGAYTAY"), and `Hillbarn Cafe` vs `Hillbarn Café` (the logo uses the acute accent, the Facebook page name does not). The website should standardise on **Hillbarn** and **Café**.

## 5. ⚠️ Conflicting opening hours — DO NOT PUBLISH UNVERIFIED

Three different schedules were found:

| Source | Mon–Fri | Sat–Sun |
|---|---|---|
| Philippine Primer | 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM | 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| MenuPH | 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM | 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| **Hillbarn's own graphic** (effectivity 13 Jan 2025) | **11:00 AM** – (cut off) | **10:00 AM** – (cut off) |

Hillbarn's own graphic is the most authoritative and the most recent, and it **contradicts both directories** on opening time. The closing times were cropped out of the available image resolution.

The same graphic also states the café is **closed every 1st Tuesday of the month** for general cleaning — a fact **no directory carries**. This alone is a reason the website matters.

## 6. ⚠️ Pricing — what is real and what is not

**No wedding or event package price published by Hillbarn was found.**

Two figures surfaced in search and are recorded here **only to prevent them being mistaken for Hillbarn's own rates**:

1. **₱228,000 for 150 pax ("Blue Rustic" package)** — this is a **caterer's** package (Juan Carlo/partner) *held at* Hillbarn. It bundles food, chairs and florals. It is **not** a Hillbarn venue rate. Do not attribute it to Hillbarn.
2. **₱88,000 venue-only introductory rate** — a promotional offer for "the first 30 couples," expiring **31 August 2021**. **Five years expired. Do not use.**

**All package and venue pricing is TBD and must come from Hillbarn directly.**

## 7. Business-model evidence

- **Accredited-caterer model, confirmed.** Hillbarn's own Instagram graphic for "The Tasting Room" (17–18 May 2025) is **co-branded with Laurentino's Catering & Events**. Juan Carlo and Town's Delight both publish Hillbarn as a venue they serve. Hillbarn supplies the *venue*; food for large events comes from partner caterers.
- **"The Tasting Room"** is a real, named Hillbarn event format — a food-tasting open house for prospective event clients, run with a caterer partner. This is a genuine conversion mechanic worth building into the site.
- The café operates a **separate, self-run F&B business** with its own menu and its own brand mark.
