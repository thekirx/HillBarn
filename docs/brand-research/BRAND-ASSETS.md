## ⚠️ Read this before using any asset

1. **Ownership.** Every asset here is **© Hillbarn** (or their commissioned photographers). They were collected from Hillbarn's own public Facebook and Instagram accounts **for brand-research and internal mock-up purposes**. They are **not licensed for public launch**. Written permission or original files must be obtained from Hillbarn before the site goes live.

2. **Resolution.** These are social-media-compressed derivatives. **Only one asset exceeds 1000 px on any edge.** None is production-grade for a full-bleed desktop hero. Every asset below carries a `web_ready` verdict.

3. **No AI-generated or stock substitutes** were created or used. Where an asset is missing, the manifest says *missing* rather than filling the gap.

**Quality legend**
`A` usable in the mock at intended size · `B` usable small or as a positioned placeholder · `C` reference/moodboard only
