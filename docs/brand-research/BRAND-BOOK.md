**Evidence-backed brand audit for Hillbarn, Tagaytay City, Philippines**
Compiled 5 September 2026 · Public sources only · See `RESEARCH-SOURCES.md`

> **🟢 VERIFIED** — observed directly in Hillbarn's own published material, and

## 1. Brand Overview

### 🟢 VERIFIED

Hillbarn is a **single Tagaytay property running two brands from one address** — Daang Luma Road, Barangay Kaybagal North.

**Hillbarn Café & Restaurant** — a Dutch-barn/windmill-themed café and restaurant. Hillbarn's own description, verbatim from its Facebook Intro:

> "Dutch barn themed cafe and restaurant in the heart of Tagaytay City"

**Hillbarn Tagaytay Events Place** — a barn-themed events and wedding venue on the same grounds.

The physical signature is a **full mock Dutch windmill** — a functioning building, not a decoration. Its silhouette is the single most recognisable thing Hillbarn owns, and it is already used as the events logo mark.

**Architecture:** gambrel-roofed (barn-profile) buildings in white/cream board siding, with **dark slate teal-navy roofs** (sampled **#293742**), white balustrades and trim, grey stone pillars, gravel and cobblestone drives. Verified via Hillbarn's own aerial cover photo and facade graphics.

**Layout** (Top Gear PH, Nov 2024): café on the ground floor; main dining on the second floor, where the interior walls slope to follow the windmill structure; additional outdoor seating at the rear and side; large gravel parking front and opposite.

**Scale:** ~5,000 sqm, up to 300 guests — ⚠️ *medium confidence, sourced from caterer partners, not from Hillbarn.*

### The two-brand problem — and the opportunity

This is the central strategic finding of the audit.

The café and the events place currently present as **two different companies**. They have **different logos**, different colour treatments, different social accounts, and no shared visual system. A couple who discovers the windmill café on Facebook has no obvious path to learning they can be married there.

| | Café & Restaurant | Events Place |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | Serif wordmark, no icon | Windmill icon + serif wordmark |
| Audience | 114K on Facebook | 4.9K on Instagram |
| Palette lead | Cream / warm black / copper | Deep teal, navy, cream |
| Locator word | "CAFÉ & RESTAURANT" | "TAGAYTAY" |

**The website's primary job is to be the first place these two brands are one brand** — without flattening either.

### 🔵 RECOMMENDED EXTENSION — Positioning

> **Hillbarn is the Tagaytay landmark you drive up for — and the one you come back to for the day that matters most.**

A destination café by day, a celebration venue by season, held together by one windmill on one hill. The site should let a visitor arrive for *lunch* and leave thinking about their *wedding*, or arrive for a *venue* and leave hungry.

**Positioning guardrails:**
- Not a farm-to-table or agritourism brand — there is no evidence of farming, produce or sustainability messaging.
- Not a Taal-view brand. **Hillbarn does not sell a volcano view**; no Hillbarn material shows or claims one. This is a genuine differentiator to work *around*, not to fake. Hillbarn's view is *its own architecture*.
- Not "rustic barn" in the American sense. The barn here is **Dutch/Northern European, painted and precise** — nearer to Scandinavian than to reclaimed-timber country.

## 3. Logo

### 🟢 VERIFIED — Hillbarn has TWO distinct marks

#### Mark A — Café & Restaurant wordmark

Text-only, stacked, centred:

```
        HILLBARN
    CAFÉ & RESTAURANT
```

- **"HILLBARN"** — all caps, high-contrast display serif, near-black warm ink, moderate letterspacing.
- **"CAFÉ & RESTAURANT"** — all caps, widely letterspaced (approx. 0.2–0.3em), noticeably smaller, set in a **warm copper/tobacco tone**, with a spelled-out ampersand.
- Set on **plain cream/white**. No icon, no container, no rule.
- Note the **acute accent on CAFÉ** in the logo — the Facebook page name omits it.

Best available: `public/brand/logo/hillbarn-wordmark-lockup-crop-470x120.jpg`, cropped from Hillbarn's own 960 px poster where the lockup is rendered large and clean in cream on wood — a far better reference than the 150 px avatar.

#### Mark B — Events Place lockup

```
        [windmill icon]
        HILLBARN
    ——  TAGAYTAY  ——
```

- A **simplified front-elevation windmill** — sails, gambrel body, door and windows — rendered in dusty slate-blue with terracotta window/door accents in the avatar version, and as **white monoline** in graphics and in the physical entrance signage.
- "HILLBARN" in a serif beneath; "TAGAYTAY" letterspaced small, **flanked by horizontal rules**.
- Verified in three independent places: the Instagram avatar, the co-branded Tasting Room graphic, and the **built entrance signage** (dimensional cream letters on dark stained wood, in a gambrel-shaped panel).

### Logo — DO NOT

- ❌ Do not redesign, redraw or "clean up" either mark.
- ❌ Do not AI-generate or trace a replacement.
- ❌ Do not merge the two marks into a new single logo.
- ❌ Do not place Mark A on a busy photo without a scrim — it is a hairline-contrast serif and it breaks up.
- ❌ Do not letterspace-adjust "CAFÉ & RESTAURANT"; the wide tracking *is* the mark.

### ⚠️ Asset status — the biggest gap in this audit

| Format | Available? |
|---|---|
| SVG | ❌ None found |
| Transparent PNG | ❌ None found |
| High-res PNG | ❌ None found |
| High-res JPG | ⚠️ Partial — 470×120 crop from a poster, and a 150×150 avatar |

**No usable production logo file exists in public sources.** The 150 px avatars are below the resolution needed for a retina site header.

**Required action:** request vector files (SVG/AI/EPS) from Hillbarn. Until then, treat all logo rendering in the mock as **placeholder at reduced fidelity**, and say so on delivery.

### 🔵 RECOMMENDED EXTENSION — a unifying architecture (proposal, not fact)

Keep both marks; give them one family:

- **Master brand:** `HILLBARN` wordmark alone in the site header.
- **Descriptor swap** below it, per section: `CAFÉ & RESTAURANT` / `TAGAYTAY · EVENTS`.
- Reserve the **windmill icon** as the favicon, app icon and section-break motif — it is the strongest single asset either arm owns.

## 5. Typography

### ⚠️ Honest statement of uncertainty

**No typeface below is confirmed.** Hillbarn publishes no brand guide, and no font files or CSS were obtainable — there is no website to inspect. Everything here is **visual classification from rendered artwork**, with recommended substitutes. **Do not tell anyone Hillbarn "uses" a named font.**

### 🟢 VERIFIED — the typographic *system*

Across four years of material, Hillbarn consistently combines **three** voices:

1. **A high-contrast display serif** — headlines and the wordmark
2. **A widely letterspaced sans or serif in all caps** — kickers, dates, meta, macros
3. **A script** — used as an emotional counterpoint on the second line

Plus a recurring fourth: **italic serif for pull-quotes and taglines** ("*Bought by the regulars, four years and counting.*", "*Made from scratch. No fillers added.*").

That four-part system is stable and genuinely distinctive. **The system is the brand asset — not any individual font.**

### Observed roles

| Role | Observed characteristics | Confidence | Closest legal web font |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / wordmark** | All caps, high contrast, vertical stress, sharp flat unbracketed serifs, slightly condensed, flat-apex A, straight-leg R | Low on identity, high on style | **Prata** (Google) or **DM Serif Display** (Google) |
| **Headline** | Same family, mixed case, tighter — "Four years. One favourite." | Low | **Playfair Display** (Google, variable) |
| **Kicker / meta** | All caps, ~0.2–0.3em tracking, small, often copper | Medium | **Jost** or **Inter** (Google), 500 wt |
| **Script accent** | ⚠️ **At least two different scripts.** A thin signature script on the aerial cover; a bold retro brush script on the burger posters | Low | **Dancing Script** (thin) / consider a single choice |
| **Italic editorial** | Serif italic, mid-weight, sentence case | Low | **Playfair Display Italic** or **Lora Italic** |
| **Body** | *Not observed* — Hillbarn has no long-form typography anywhere | n/a | **Inter** or **Source Serif 4** |

### ⚠️ Inconsistencies found (opportunities, not criticisms)

- **Two different scripts** in active use.
- **"favourite" vs "favorite"** — both spellings appear on two versions of the *same* campaign.
- **"Hillbarn" vs "Hill Barn"**.

The website should **choose one of each** and become the reference.

### 🔵 RECOMMENDED EXTENSION — web type scale

**Stack** (all open-licence, self-hostable):
- **Display/Headline:** Playfair Display (variable 400–900) — closest to the observed high-contrast serif, and it has a genuine italic for the pull-quote role.
- **Kicker/UI/Body:** Inter (variable) — neutral, tracks well at wide letterspacing.
- **Script:** **one** only, used **sparingly** — reserved for the events arm.

```
Display XL   clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 7rem)     Playfair 500   line-height 1.02  tracking -0.02em
Display L    clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)   Playfair 500   1.08             -0.015em
Heading M    clamp(1.75rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)  Playfair 500   1.15             -0.01em
Heading S    1.375rem                     Playfair 600   1.25              0
Kicker       0.75rem                      Inter 500      1.4        UPPERCASE 0.24em
Body L       1.125rem                     Inter 400      1.7               0
Body         1rem                         Inter 400      1.65              0
Caption      0.8125rem                    Inter 400      1.5               0.01em
Pull-quote   clamp(1.25rem,2vw,1.75rem)   Playfair Italic 400  1.5         0
```

**Case rules (from observed practice):**
- Wordmark and kickers: **UPPERCASE**, wide tracking.
- Headlines: **sentence case** — Hillbarn writes "Four years. One favourite.", not "FOUR YEARS".
- Never uppercase a full sentence of body copy.

## 6. Photography

### 🟢 VERIFIED — three distinct photographic modes

**A. Food — studio-controlled**
Two sub-styles, both in current use:
- *Light:* white plate, pale ground, soft diffuse light, near-overhead or 45°. Shallow depth. Garnish is deliberate — pink peppercorns, micro-herbs, basil, halved cherry tomatoes.
- *Dark:* dark stained wood surface, directional warm light, deep shadow, cream/ivory type reversed out.

**B. Venue & architecture — golden hour, aerial**
The cover photo is a **drone shot at golden hour**: amber sky, silhouetted palms and pines, windmill catching the last light. Strongly warm-graded, lifted shadows, gentle vignette.

**C. Events & weddings — warm ambient, editorial**
The reception image is the single richest asset found: elevated three-quarter view of the barn interior — polished concrete floor, exposed timber trusses, **suspended floral installations in coral/orange and deep green**, festoon and Edison string lights, long timber banquet tables, round-back cane chairs, tan napkins, a circular white stage, tan leather lounge sofa. Warm tungsten ambience, deep shadow, saturated florals.

**Grade characteristics across all modes:**

| Attribute | Reading |
|---|---|
| Temperature | **Warm** throughout — amber/gold highlights, warm shadows |
| Saturation | **Moderate**, lifted in food and florals; never neon |
| Contrast | Medium-high; shadows retain detail rather than crushing |
| Lighting | Natural or warm-tungsten; **no cool white, no flash-look** |
| Depth of field | Consistently shallow in food and lifestyle |
| ⚠️ Exception | The burger posters use a **cool green bokeh** background — the one cool-toned outlier |

### Photography — DO NOT

- ❌ **Do not use stock photography of other venues.** Hillbarn's architecture is unique and unmistakable — a generic barn wedding photo will read as a lie.
- ❌ Do not apply cool, desaturated or "moody grey" grading.
- ❌ Do not crop the windmill out of establishing shots.
- ❌ Do not upscale the 414 px social assets for hero use.

### ⚠️ Critical constraint — asset resolution

| Tier | Max resolution found |
|---|---|
| Best single asset | 2048 × 824 (aerial cover) — **but only 824 px tall** |
| Food posters | 960 × 1200 |
| Most social assets | 414 × 414 to 640 × 640 |

**Nothing collected is production-grade for a full-bleed desktop hero.** The aerial is wide enough but not tall enough; everything else is small.

**This must be stated on delivery.** The mock can use these as **positioned placeholders at correct crop and grade**, but launch requires original files from Hillbarn — or a commissioned shoot. The brief for that shoot is in `WEBSITE-DIRECTION.md`.

## 7. Graphic Language

### 🟢 VERIFIED — Hillbarn's own compositional grammar

This is remarkably consistent and is the most transferable thing in the whole audit.

**The layout formula, observed repeatedly:**

```
        KICKER IN LETTERSPACED CAPS
        Display Serif Headline
        Script or italic second line
   ┌─────────────────────────────┐
   │                             │
   │        PHOTOGRAPH           │  ← often inset with cream margin,
   │                    ( badge )│    not full-bleed
   └─────────────────────────────┘
     Italic serif caption line
     META IN LETTERSPACED CAPS
```

**Recurring devices:**

| Device | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Inset image with cream margin** | The editorial poster frames the photo with generous cream on all sides — a *paper* logic, not a screen one |
| **Circular seal badge** | "BEST SELLER / No. 1 / SINCE MAY 2022" — dark or copper disc, caps arc, numeral centred |
| **Rectangular outline badge** | "Best Seller / SINCE MAY 2022" in a hairline box |
| **Horizontal rules flanking text** | `—— TAGAYTAY ——` in the events lockup |
| **Date/season kickers** | "MAY 2022 - 2026", "MAY 17 - 18, 2025 \| SAT - SUN" |
| **Data as ornament** | GPS coordinates in a corner; burger macros as a footer strip |
| **Centred symmetry** | Nearly every composition is centre-axis |

**Texture:** minimal. Flat colour fields, no drop shadows, no gradients, no grunge. The texture comes from **photography and wood**, not from graphic effects.

**Logo placement:** top-centre, small, above the headline — or bottom-centre as a sign-off. Never a corner watermark.

### 🔵 RECOMMENDED EXTENSION — translating this to the web

The single most important translation rule:

> **Hillbarn designs like print. The website should keep the paper logic.**

| Hillbarn's device | Web translation |
|---|---|
| Inset image on cream margin | Sections where images sit **within** a cream field, not edge-to-edge — a deliberate counter-move to the full-bleed default |
| Circular seal badge | Reusable badge component for "Best Seller", "Since 2022", capacity figures |
| Letterspaced caps kicker | The `eyebrow` above every section heading |
| Flanking rules | Section dividers and the events descriptor |
| Data as ornament | Publish the **GPS coordinates** in the footer — it is genuinely theirs |
| Centred symmetry | Centre-axis for hero and section intros; asymmetric only in editorial pairs |

## 8. Brand Voice

### 🟢 VERIFIED — from published captions and graphic copy

**Corpus available:** limited by logged-out access. Confident on graphic copy; **less confident on long-form captions** — only one full Facebook caption was readable.

**Observed samples:**

| Copy | Source |
|---|---|
| "Dutch barn themed cafe and restaurant in the heart of Tagaytay City" | FB bio |
| "Pork chop lovers! Our signature Hillbarn Pork Chop is a certified crowd favorite. Try it now!" | FB post, 18 July |
| "Four years. One favourite." | 2026 poster |
| "Bought by the regulars, four years and counting." | same |
| "Loved by new and regular customers" | poster variant |
| "Made from scratch. No fillers added." | burger posters |
| "PERFECT SETTING FOR YOUR WEDDING" | events Reel |
| "The Tasting Room — Deluxe Selection" | events graphic |
| "Note: Closed every 1st Tuesday of the month due to gen[eral cleaning]" | schedule graphic |

### Voice analysis

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Formal ↔ casual** | **Mid, leaning warm-casual.** Graphics are composed and formal; social captions are friendly and exclamatory |
| **Aspirational ↔ practical** | **Both, cleanly separated.** Graphics aspire; captions are practical ("Try it now!") |
| **English / Taglish** | **English throughout in Hillbarn's own voice.** ⚠️ Taglish appears in *customer comments* ("Ang sarap ng mga food nmn"), not in brand copy. **Do not write Taglish for Hillbarn** — it is not their register |
| **Emoji** | **None observed** in Hillbarn's own copy. Notably restrained |
| **Sentence length** | Very short. Fragments are common and deliberate: "Four years. One favourite." |
| **Exclamation marks** | Present in social captions, absent from graphics |

**How they describe food:** by **provenance and process**, not adjective-stacking. "Made from scratch. No fillers added." They cite **track record** as proof — "Best Seller since May 2022", "four years and counting". They name dishes with the brand attached: "**The** Hillbarn Pork Chop".

**How they describe the venue:** by **theme and singularity** — "Dutch barn themed", "in the heart of Tagaytay City". The claim "first and only barn-themed venue in Tagaytay" comes from a **caterer partner, not Hillbarn** — ⚠️ do not put it in Hillbarn's mouth.

**How they sell events:** ⚠️ **thinly.** "Perfect setting for your wedding" is close to the whole argument. This is the weakest part of the existing brand voice and the biggest copy opportunity.

**CTA style:** short, imperative, low-pressure — "Try it now!". No urgency tactics, no discounting language.

### 🔵 RECOMMENDED EXTENSION — Voice Guide

**Principles**

1. **Let the record do the boasting.** Prefer "Best seller since May 2022" over "award-winning". This is already their instinct — extend it.
2. **Short sentences. Fragments allowed.** Match "Four years. One favourite."
3. **Say the true concrete thing.** Coordinates, opening times, the cleaning-day closure, guest counts. Their precision is charming — keep it.
4. **English, no emoji, no Taglish** in brand copy.
5. **Warm imperatives for CTAs.** "Reserve a table." "Come and see it." Never "Book now!!!" or countdown urgency.
6. **Never invent heritage.** There is **no public founding story**. Do not write one. The verifiable time anchor is "**Best Seller since May 2022**" — use that and nothing more until Hillbarn supplies history.

**Words to use:** windmill · barn · Tagaytay · from scratch · regulars · table · celebration · the day · setting · grounds

**Words to avoid:** rustic-chic · elegant affair · nestled · unforgettable journey · culinary excellence · hidden gem · bespoke · luxurious · world-class

**Sample copy in-voice** *(ours — clearly marked as proposed, not Hillbarn's existing copy)*

- Hero: **"The windmill on Daang Luma."** / Sub: "A Dutch barn café in Tagaytay — and the place where the day happens."
- Dining: **"Made from scratch. Served all day."**
- Signature: **"The Hillbarn Pork Chop."** / "Best seller since May 2022."
- Events: **"Three hundred guests. One barn. Your day."** ⚠️ *capacity requires confirmation before use*
- Weddings: **"Say it under the windmill."**
- Visit CTA: **"Come and see the grounds."**

- Use **cream `#F3EFE4` as the page ground**, not white.
